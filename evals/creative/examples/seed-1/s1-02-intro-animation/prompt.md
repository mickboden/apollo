Design and build an opening animation for "Luro Collective" that plays once when the page loads and settles on the first screen. Output ./out/index.html.

This is an unattended evaluation run. Nobody will answer questions or rate a board. Where you would ask the person for facts, use the facts below. Every other decision (concept, direction, references, dials) is yours as the designer: make it and continue. Do not push, deploy or publish anything.

Facts:
- Brand: Luro Collective flies drones over farms to spot crop stress early. It started as a hobby and grew by word of mouth. (Fictional.)
- Audience: agronomists.
- Main action: schedule a survey flight.
- What the brand deals with: field health maps, flight schedule and cost per hectare.
- Personality: nerdy and meticulous.
- Constraint: type only: no pictures or illustrations, typography carries everything.
- Length: it plays once and lasts at most 2 seconds.

Output rules:
- One self-contained file, ./out/index.html, under 250 KB, with its CSS and JS inline.
- Fonts from Google Fonts only.
- Images only as inline SVG, CSS or data: URIs; no other image files and no links to images.
- Responsive from a 390px phone to a 1440px desktop.
