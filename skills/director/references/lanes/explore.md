# Lane: Explore

"Show me what's possible." References and directions, no build. The output is a rated board and a clear next step.

## Steps

1. **Load** context. Time estimate in one line.
2. **Frame** the exploration in one line: what is being explored, for whom, at which creativity level. If `superpowers:brainstorming` is installed, use it to sharpen the frame, without an extra question round.
3. **References.** As in [new.md](new.md) §3: about 10, spanning a real range, each with a specific `why`. Board them as a `refs` deck with the right `layer`, and let the person rate.
4. **Read the ratings.** In two or three lines, say what won and why in the person's terms. Save the lessons (`cli.mjs learn`).
5. **Directions** (optional, when the person wants more than references): brief **screens** for 3 truly different concept mockups plus a wild card, built from the ♥ references. Run lint and render on them; board them as `concepts`.
6. **Close** with the next step: "Pick one and I'll take it into the New lane", or "These ratings are saved; your next home screen will start from them."

## Notes

- Explore never edits project code.
- Ratings are the product of this lane. Make the cards worth rating: real screenshots, real products, one sharp `why` each.
- When the person rates references from other brands low across the board, say so; it usually means the answer is the product's own language, not a borrowed look.
